Pragmata

Rating: 4/5

Pragmata is a mysterious sci-fi adventure about a girl and an astronaut trying to survive on a ruined Earth. The game offers an interesting setting and an action-focused experience.

Phantom Blade 0

Rating: 4.5/5

Phantom Blade 0 is a fast-paced martial-arts action game set in a dark world. Players who enjoy challenging combat and action games may enjoy its style and gameplay.

Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight

Rating: 4/5

Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight is an action-packed adventure where Batman faces new villains across Gotham. Its Lego style gives the game a fun and accessible feel.

Marvel Wolverine

Rating: 4.5/5

Marvel Wolverine follows Wolverine as he takes on dangerous threats with fast and brutal combat. It is aimed at players who enjoy superhero action games.

Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced

Rating: 4/5

Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced brings a pirate adventure with exploration, sailing, and hidden secrets. Players can explore the seas while taking on enemies and discovering new locations.
